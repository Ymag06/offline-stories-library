The Hare and the Tortoise

One day, a hare and a tortoise met each other. The hare teased the tortoise and said, “Golden Tortoise, you walk so slowly!”

The tortoise replied, “Golden Hare, would you like to have a race with me? I’m not afraid of you.”

The next morning, the hare and the tortoise began their race.

The hare ran very fast, while the tortoise kept moving slowly without stopping. When the hare reached a shady place, he thought, “I’m already close to the finish line. I’ll take a little rest while I wait for the tortoise.” So, he lay down and fell asleep.

When the hare woke up, he immediately got up and started running. But it was too late. The tortoise had already reached the finish line first.

The hare thought regretfully, “I underestimated the tortoise. If I had kept trying with the same determination as the tortoise, I would have reached the finish line first.”

 Lesson

**Never underestimate others. Keep trying and never give up, even if you are moving slowly.**
